portly

A personal browser home page that runs on your own PC or in your own browser

Portly replaces the browser's New Tab page with a dashboard of search, bookmarks, calendar, mail, tasks and weather. It runs entirely on its owner's computer, or as a browser-only site that keeps its data in the browser — there is no Portly cloud service and no Portly account.

What it does

  • Search across several search engines from one box.
  • Launch pad of bookmark groups with icons.
  • Calendar — shows Google Calendar events and lets you create, edit and delete them, alongside other calendars you connect.
  • Mail — shows the unread count and the most recent inbox messages (sender, subject, date and a short preview) from Gmail and other mailboxes, and lets you mark them read or unread, archive them, or move them to the trash.
  • Tasks from Todoist, weather, sports scores, RSS feeds and a clock.

How it uses your Google account

Portly connects to Google with OAuth, only after you sign in and approve access on Google's consent screen. It requests:

  • your email address and basic profile, to label the connected account;
  • your calendar list (read-only) and calendar events (read and write), to show and edit your calendar;
  • Gmail modify access, to show your recent inbox messages and to mark read/unread, archive and trash. Portly never sends mail and never permanently deletes mail.

Everything Portly receives from Google stays on the PC where Portly runs. It is not sent to any server operated by Portly, shared with anyone, or used for advertising or AI training. Details are in the privacy policy.

Who can use it

Portly is a personal project by davideAurum. It is self-hosted: each person who runs it uses their own copy on their own computer or in their own browser, with their own Google Cloud OAuth client and Microsoft app registration. It is not offered as a hosted service.
