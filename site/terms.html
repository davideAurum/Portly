What Homebase is

Homebase is personal, self-hosted software developed by davideAurum. It runs on your own computer and connects to accounts you choose (such as Google Calendar and Gmail) using your own credentials. It is not a hosted service, and there is no Homebase account to create.

Your responsibilities

  • Use Homebase only with accounts you own or are authorized to use.
  • Keep the computer Homebase runs on secure; Homebase stores access tokens on it.
  • Your use of Google services through Homebase remains subject to Google's Terms of Service, and the same applies to other providers you connect.

Actions on your data

Homebase acts on your calendar and mail only when you ask it to — for example creating or deleting an event, or archiving or trashing a message. Those changes happen in your account at the provider, as if you had made them yourself.
