What Portly is

Portly is personal, self-hosted software developed by davideAurum. It runs on your own computer, or as a browser-only site that keeps its data in your browser, and its browser extension can make it your New Tab page. It connects to accounts you choose (such as Google Calendar, Gmail and Outlook.com) using your own credentials. It is not a hosted service, and there is no Portly account to create.

Your responsibilities

  • Use Portly only with accounts you own or are authorized to use.
  • Keep the computer Portly runs on, and the browser you use it in, secure; Portly stores access tokens there.
  • Your use of Google services through Portly remains subject to Google's Terms of Service, and the same applies to other providers you connect.

Actions on your data

Portly acts on your calendar, mail and tasks only when you ask it to — for example creating or deleting an event, archiving or trashing a message, or completing a task. Those changes happen in your account at the provider, as if you had made them yourself.
